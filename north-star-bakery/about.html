Our Story

North Star Bakery is a neighborhood bakery built with the passion for fresh, handmade baked goods and to bring people together. We are proud to serve our community with breads, pastries, and cakes made with care.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe baked goods should start with quality ingredients. North Star Bakery focuses on carefully selected ingredients and a homemade approach to create goods for our community.

Meet Our Team

Our team works together to prepare fresh baked goods, while providing a welcoming experience for our neighborhood.
